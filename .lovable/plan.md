## Templated / dynamic lines
- All templates (e.g. "Catch the letter {X}!", "Spell {word} to launch the rocket!", "Find {X}. Tap every one you can see.") were counted as every real sentence they produce, same as the tracing batch.
- Cannot be pre-recorded: "Hello {child's name}! Let's play." (typed name). Only "Hello friend!" is counted; named greetings keep using the device voice or get recorded the first time a signed-in family hears them.
- 160 rhyme hints contain "…" which the voice filter rejects, so they always use the device voice. Optional small fix: swap "…" for a comma.

## Recommendation: split praise from reveal/hint (component clips)
Instead of recording every combination, play the short praise clip (already recorded) followed by the reveal or hint clip.

| Approach | Lines to record | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Full sentences (as the app speaks today) | 14,575 | 460,692 |
| Component clips (praise and reveal/hint played back to back) | 4,854 | **~102,950** |

Component breakdown: 1,283 reveals (25,570) + 937 hints (21,005) + 2,634 other full lines (56,372). Praise clips are already recorded.

Tradeoff: a tiny pause between "Great job!" and "B is for bear!", and the praise intonation won't flow into the reveal. For ages 2–6 this sounds natural (praise, then the teaching line). It needs a small change to the narration code so two clips play one after another; no change to scoring, games, sign-in, payments or the grown-up check. Other templated lines (prompts, "You found X!") stay as full sentences; splitting them would sound choppy and saves little.

Optional staging if ~103k credits is too much at once:
1. New since last batches (six bespoke games, Hunt, tracing gaps, Memory, flash backs, Story Builder, onboarding): ~590 lines, ~22,500 credits.
2. All question prompts + Word Finds: ~1,800 lines, ~33,800 credits.
3. Reveals and hints (component clips): ~2,220 lines, ~46,600 credits.

Reminder: signed-in families already fill the library automatically as they play (capped per hour), so anything not pre-recorded still gets recorded over time.
