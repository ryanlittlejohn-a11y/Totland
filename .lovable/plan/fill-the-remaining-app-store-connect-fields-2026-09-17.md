# Fill the remaining App Store Connect fields

## What's blocking review

None of these are app problems — they are form fields and upload timing in App Store Connect:

1. **"Preview uploads in progress"** — the App Preview videos you just uploaded are still being processed by Apple's servers. This clears on its own; it can take anywhere from a few minutes to a few hours per video. No action needed beyond waiting and refreshing the page.
2. **English (U.S.) – Subtitle (required)** — a one-line tagline (max 30 characters) for the App Store listing, under App Information / the version page. Proposed text to paste in: **"Learn by playing"** (18 characters).
3. **English (U.S.) – Action (required)** — this is the App Clip card's button label shown when the clip launches. Proposed text to paste in: **"Play"**.
4. **English (U.S.) – Header Image (required)** — the App Clip card's header image. Apple requires exactly **3000×2000 px**, PNG or JPEG, no transparency.
