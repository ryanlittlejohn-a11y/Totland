## Verified — 2026-10-09

1. **Mascot and illustration artwork** (app icon, splash art, character
   illustrations) — generated via **Lovable** under the owner's Lovable
   account. Lovable's Terms of Service (https://lovable.dev/terms, "Your
   Rights"): *"As between us, you own your Customer Data, including the
   applications... you also own any AI Output generated for you through the
   Services, subject to any third-party rights in the underlying models,
   training data, or outputs."* — **Commercial use: Yes.** Caveat carried
   forward from Lovable's own Terms: AI Output "may be similar or identical
   to content generated for other users," and ownership is subject to any
   third-party rights in the output. Mitigated by Totland's own build rule to
   avoid designs that imitate recognizable existing copyrighted characters.
   Still TODO: keep the Lovable generation history/exports as a dated record
   per asset.

2. **Background music — three tracks** ("Little Steps, Big Dreams", "Curious
   Steps", "Forest of Wonder") — generated with **ElevenLabs Music** on a paid
   plan (Starter, then Creator). ElevenLabs help center
   (https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform):
   *"All paid plans include a commercial license... content generated during
   a paid subscription can be used commercially, and indefinitely."*
   Eleven Music Service Terms: Starter/Creator+ plans restrict Outputs from
   **offline** (non-internet/non-app) TV/radio/film/live-event use, high-traffic
   public events, and bulk third-party licensing/distribution — none of which
   apply to music played back inside Totland's own mobile app.
   **Commercial use: Yes, for in-app playback.** Do NOT release these tracks
   as standalone streaming-platform singles (Spotify/Apple Music/etc.) or
   license them out — that crosses into the restricted use cases.
   Still TODO: file the dated receipt/plan-tier proof for each generation.

3. **"Hannah" narration voice** (`ZSNL4hPqCnqoMPaI4jGX`, ElevenLabs Voice
   Library, `eleven_multilingual_v2`) — commercial use of *output* is covered
   by the same paid-plan commercial license as above. Voice Library terms
   add a separate wrinkle: Library voices carry the *original voice owner's*
   sharing terms, and if that owner ever stops sharing the voice, ElevenLabs
   gives account holders advance notice (up to a 2-year notice period) before
   access ends — so this is an operational continuity risk, not an
   infringement risk. **Commercial use: Yes, subject to notice-period churn.**
   Still TODO: (a) confirm this specific voice's listing allows commercial use
   (not every Library voice does — some are restricted to higher tiers or
   excluded from commercial use by the owner), (b) note the voice's current
   notice-period length, (c) keep a fallback plan (re-record narration or
   switch voices) if "Hannah" is ever pulled.
